# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Followers of Uebert Angel Jr., spiritually curious visitors, and prospective members of The Realm of Seers. Many arrive on mobile from social media and need to understand the purpose of the community, explore its teaching, and reach the membership destination without friction.

## Product Purpose

Present The Realm of Seers as the official teaching and community platform of Uebert Angel Jr. The site should communicate its spiritual purpose, establish trust through real imagery and teaching material, and guide visitors toward joining the private community.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, reverent, premium. The experience should feel spiritually serious and visually assured, with disciplined movement, rich darkness, and a restrained red accent.

## Anti-references

Generic ministry templates, noisy neon effects, excessive rounded cards, overlapping copy, horizontal-scroll glitches, animation for its own sake, and navigation that behaves differently across devices.

## Design Principles

1. Let authentic imagery carry the emotional weight.
2. Keep every interaction direct, stable, and easy to understand.
3. Use motion to guide attention without moving the page sideways.
4. Preserve a clear path from discovery to community membership.
5. Maintain cinematic atmosphere without sacrificing readability.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Maintain readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic landmarks, 44px minimum touch targets, descriptive alternative text, and complete navigation without a pointer. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by removing non-essential movement and smooth-scroll animation while preserving all content and functionality.
